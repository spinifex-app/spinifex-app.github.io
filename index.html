Getting started

Spinifex is an app for recording plant and animal observations in the field in Victoria, Australia, with a built-in list of Victorian species categorised by CMA. The species list covers plants, amphibians, birds, mammals and reptiles.

  1. Choose your region. Select your state and Catchment Management Authority (CMA) region. You can change these at any time in Settings.
  2. Record an observation. In the Species box, start typing any part of a common or scientific name. The list filters to species recorded in your CMA. Tap the correct species to select it.
  3. Add details (optional). Enter cover values and comments for each record. To tag records to a particular reserve, monitoring quadrat or site, type its name in the Site box. Every new record will carry that site name until you change or clear it.
  4. Submit your record. Tap the Submit button to save your observation to your saved records list.

Tip: Under the GPS coordinates, the status shows “Tracking GPS location” in green when your location is accurate to within 50 metres. If it turns red and shows “GPS location not accurate”, your device doesn’t have a good location fix yet. Wait for it to turn green before saving a record.

Review or edit your records

Tap the list icon in the top-right corner to see your saved records. You can edit them from there.

Export your data

On the saved records screen, tap the export icon in the top-right corner to generate a CSV of your records. From there, choose to save the file to your device or share it by email, message, AirDrop or another app. The CSV includes your entries, location data and timestamps, plus extra columns for each species such as origin, taxon ID, conservation status and lifeform.

Clear your list

To start fresh, tap the delete icon in the top-right corner of the saved records screen. This clears all your data, so export first, because clearing can’t be undone.

Subscription and billing

Price and billing: Spinifex is an annual subscription of $30.00 AUD per year. New subscribers get a 14-day free trial. After the trial, your Apple ID is charged $30.00 AUD unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically each year unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

To manage or cancel: On your iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Spinifex and choose Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Restore a purchase: If you’ve reinstalled the app or changed phones, sign in with the Apple ID you used to subscribe and open the app. Your subscription should be recognised automatically. If it isn’t, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

Refunds: Payments are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Subscriptions are handled directly through your Apple ID.
Where are my records stored?
Only on your device, or in the CSV files you export.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Spinifex works fully offline. You can record species observations without an internet connection, because all species data is stored on your device. You can also export your data and save it to your device without reception, but you’ll need a connection to email or message the file.
I found an error in the species list.
Email spinifexapp@gmail.com with the species name and what looks wrong, and we’ll check it.

About the data

Spinifex’s built-in species information comes from the Victorian Biodiversity Atlas, downloaded in January 2025 and adapted for use in the app.
