Who we are

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information the app collects, how it’s used, and your choices.

Information the app collects

The records you create in the app (such as species, dates, notes and locations) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If you export your records as a CSV file, the file is saved or shared wherever you choose; we do not receive it. If you use iCloud or other device backups, your app data may be included in them, depending on your own device settings.

The only information that leaves your device is the subscription information described under “Payments and subscriptions” below.

Location

The app uses your device’s location to record where species observations are made. Location is stored only on your device. You can turn off location access at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Payments and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. When you start a trial, subscribe, restore a purchase or open the app, RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase information (such as the product, purchase and expiry dates, and whether you’re in a trial), a random anonymous ID created by the app, and basic technical details such as your device type, app version and country. This is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID, and it does not include your observation records or location. RevenueCat uses it only to tell the app whether your subscription is active. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Apple’s own privacy policy applies to your payments.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone other than Apple and RevenueCat for the subscription purposes described above.

Species data

The app’s built-in species list comes from the Victorian Biodiversity Atlas (VBA), which is owned and managed by the Victorian Department of Energy, Environment and Climate Action (DEECA). We downloaded the data in January 2025 and have adapted it for use in the app by removing columns, changing column names and grouping species based on records in CMA regions. It is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). Spinifex is an independent app and is not endorsed by, or affiliated with, DEECA or the Victorian Government.

The species list is general reference information and contains no personal information about you. Observations you record in the app stay on your device and are not submitted to the VBA. Full credit and licence details are in the app’s Settings and on our Support page.

Keeping information secure

Your records are stored on your device and are protected by your device’s security settings.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because your records are stored on your device, you can delete them at any time by clearing your saved records within the app. Exported CSV files are stored wherever you saved them and can be deleted there.

We don’t hold any personal information about you. The anonymous subscription record kept by RevenueCat isn’t linked to your identity; if you’d like it deleted, email spinifexapp@gmail.com.

If you have a question about this policy, email spinifexapp@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
